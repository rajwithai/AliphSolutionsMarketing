**CTA:** Browse Templates

### Tier 2 — Light Customization

### Dashboard Walkthrough (Plain‑English)

1. **Compliance Score** — One glance shows overall status (Green/Amber/Red). It’s based on the templates and checklists you’ve chosen and the answers you provided. **No system integrations are required to get started.**
2. **Open Actions & Owners** — Your next 5 tasks with due dates and responsible owners. Click to assign, @mention, or request an expert review.
3. **Board & Committee Calendar** — Upload agendas, attach charters, and export minutes. Add to Outlook/Google with one click (iCal). **No IT needed.**
4. **Risk Heatmap** — Select your top risks; the platform auto‑calculates a simple heatmap. Export to PPT for your next Audit Committee.
5. **Policies & Controls Status** — See which policies are ready, which need review, and what evidence is missing.
6. **KSA Reg Updates** — A curated stream of CMA/SAMA/MOC/PDPL developments translated into plain English with action prompts.
7. **Expert Chat & Handoff** — Escalate any item to Tier 2/3. You’ll see named experts, ETA, and fixed fees before you approve.
8. **Export Center** — Download Word/PDF/PPT packs with your logo and metadata.

### Why Our Tech Isn’t “Complex”

* **No‑code onboarding:** Start with forms, checklists, and guided drafting.
* **Optional integrations:** Add SSO, DMS, or ticketing later—only if useful.
* **Your data stays yours:** Residency options, encryption at rest/in transit.
* **Clear “what the AI did” logs:** Every suggestion cites the underlying checklist, template, or rule.
* **Humans in the loop:** High‑stakes artifacts get rapid expert QA when you ask.

### Onboarding in 24 Hours (No IT Required)

* **Day 0 (20 minutes):** Intake form → choose Starter Pack.
* **Day 1:** Workspace provisioned; your first dashboard live; top 5 actions generated.
* **By 72 hours:** A board‑ready policy/charter reviewed via Tier 2 (typical).
* **Week 1:** Risk heatmap and compliance score trending.

### What Happens Behind the Scenes (Simple Terms)

Expert‑built templates + Saudi rule packs → AI drafts the first version → you answer clarifying questions → optional expert review → clean exports. **Nothing is auto‑sent**—you approve everything.

### Security & Privacy (Plain Language)

Encryption, role‑based access, audit trail on changes, NDAs for experts, and a signed processing addendum on request. You can delete your data at any time.

### Common Objections → Our Answers

* **“Will this replace our counsel?”** No. We shorten cycles and reduce costs; complex matters still use your counsel—often through our Tier 3.
* **“Is AI risky?”** We don’t auto‑file or auto‑send. You review, and experts QA when needed.
* **“How much training?”** 30‑minute live demo covers 90% of use. Short videos are built into each screen.
* **“Vendor lock‑in?”** Export everything (Word/PDF/PPT/CSV); no proprietary file traps.

### Quick Comparison

* **Time to first draft:** Hours with Aliph vs. weeks with proposals.
* **Visibility:** Live dashboard vs. static emails.
* **Cost control:** Published prices and fixed milestones vs. open‑ended T\&M.
