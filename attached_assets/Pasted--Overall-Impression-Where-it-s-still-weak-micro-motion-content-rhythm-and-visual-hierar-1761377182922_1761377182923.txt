## 🎨 **Design & Visual Feedback**

### ✅ What’s Working

* Colors per tier (blue/green/purple) — balanced and easy to track.
* Gradient progress bars — intuitive visual for “time to completion.”
* “Seamless Progression” tag — smart and subtle.
* The CTA “Explore All Tiers” centered below — right place.

### ⚙️ **Refinements Needed**

1. **Unify the Tier Cards Height:**

   * All three cards should be **equal height**, even if Tier 1 has less text.
   * This keeps a clean horizontal line visually across screens.

2. **Add Connection Flow (Visual Continuity):**

   * Replace the two **backward arrows (←)** with **forward flow arrows (→)** or connecting curved gradient lines between tiers.
   * Currently, backward arrows visually suggest regression.
   * A simple fade line or animated gradient connector (Framer Motion path) works great.

3. **Enhance Hover/Active States:**

   * On hover → subtle card lift (4–6 px) + shadow glow in tier color.
   * Optional: When a user hovers, progress bar animates filling to 100%.

4. **Background / Context:**

   * Very light grid texture is fine; maybe drop opacity 10–15% more — it’s slightly busy behind text.
   * Consider adding a faint top–bottom gradient (#F9FAFF → #FFFFFF) for depth.

5. **CTA Section (“Explore All Tiers”):**

   * Rename to **“Compare the Tiers”** (more relevant; “Explore” implies new page).
   * On click → smooth scroll to this same section’s top or show modal (comparison chart) — avoid dead-end.
   * Make button text bold with a small right arrow → “Compare the Tiers →”.
